02 / THE METHOD

Four steps to a decision.

Attest organizes the release evidence. Your team still owns what ships.

  1. 01

    Bring two builds and declarations

    Provide the last shipped APK or AAB, the candidate, and current Data Safety, privacy policy, and listing material.

  2. 02

    Inspect the delta and truth gaps

    See new permissions, SDKs, and destinations alongside the exact declaration or claim that needs another look.

  3. 03

    Record or compare a Reviewer Twin

    On an Android device, a person follows each reviewer step. Attest captures expected and observed screens, then finds the first changed or failed step.

  4. 04

    Make a human release decision

    Review the Passport’s SHIP, REVIEW, or HOLD recommendation. Record a decision or time-boxed exception, then seal the evidence.

04 / THE BOUNDARY

The evidence stays local.
The decision stays human.

Attest runs on your machine and inspects compiled APKs or AABs; source code is not required. Reviewer credentials are referenced by label and expiry, not stored as secrets in the journey or Capsule.

Its recommendation is evidence-backed, not an approval. Findings stay visible when an exception is accepted. A person records SHIP or HOLD, and an override needs a reason.

06 / COMMON QUESTIONS

Before you run it.

What is a “truth gap”?

A mismatch between what the build shows and what the release materials say—or a claim without supporting evidence. Attest names the sources in conflict so a person can investigate.

Does Attest upload my builds?

The CLI runs locally. Its core inspection and verification do not require uploading an artifact or source code to Attest.

Is Reviewer Twin automated?

Recording is human-guided today. You follow the steps on an Android device over ADB; Attest records screenshots and compares the new run with an approved one.

Does HOLD prevent a release?

It is a recommendation and a CI gate you can configure. A person makes the final decision. If they ship against HOLD, Attest requires an explicit reason in the recorded decision.
